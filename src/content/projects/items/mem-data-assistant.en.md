## Background

A team project (in progress) for the Engineering Management (MEM) course "AI + Engineering Management", themed **data analytics and processing assistant for enterprise management**. The 7-member team spans engineering, data analytics, taxation, and finance backgrounds. Our chosen topic: **social-insurance and individual-income-tax compliance analytics** — helping enterprises surface compliance risks such as inconsistent contribution bases from payroll and tax-filing data.

## My Responsibilities

- **Technical design**: overall data-processing pipeline and system architecture
- **Data-processing module**: cleaning, standardization, and match-merge of multi-source social-insurance and tax data
- **Rule-engine module**: implementing compliance checks (contribution bases, coverage, consistency validation) as a configurable rule engine — making rules visible, editable, and traceable

## Status

In the design and module-development phase (since Sep 2026); to be delivered as a course capstone defense.
